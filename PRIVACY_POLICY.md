# ToolVault AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

ToolVault AI is designed as an offline-friendly tool inventory app. The app stores tool records, photos, maintenance logs, assignment notes, theft reports, and generated report data locally on your device using Apple's local storage technologies.

## Data Collection

ToolVault AI does not collect personal data from this app and does not sell personal data.

The app does not require an account or sign-in. Information you enter into the app, including tool names, serial numbers, assigned worker names, notes, photos, receipts, maintenance history, and resale estimates, remains on your device unless you choose to export, share, or back it up through Apple or another service outside the app.

## Camera, Photos, Notifications, and Files

ToolVault AI may request access to the camera or photo library when you choose to add tool photos or receipts. These images are used for inventory and mock AI scanner workflows inside the app.

ToolVault AI may request notification permission so it can send local maintenance reminders. These reminders are generated on your device.

When you export a PDF report or share app data, you control where that exported file is sent.

## AI Estimates

Mock AI is enabled by default in the current app build. AI-generated condition summaries, resale ranges, and inventory insights are informational estimates only. They are not insurance valuations, guarantees, or financial advice.

Future versions may offer optional remote AI features. If remote AI features are added, this policy will be updated before those features collect or transmit app data.

## Subscriptions

ToolVault AI may offer subscriptions through Apple's StoreKit system. Purchases, renewals, cancellations, and payment information are handled by Apple. ToolVault AI does not receive your payment card details.

## Tracking and Advertising

ToolVault AI does not track users across apps or websites owned by other companies. The app does not include third-party advertising.

## Children's Privacy

ToolVault AI is intended for tradespeople, contractors, technicians, and business users. It is not directed to children.
